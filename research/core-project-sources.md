## Insurance — The Commercial Anchor Vertical

### The Offensive Case (Why Insurance Co-Develops Now)
- **Swiss Re / AXA:** AI declared #1 strategic priority. AXA Future Risks Report 2025 — behavioral and social signals = top emerging risk indicators.
- **Munich Re:** Founding member of QUTAC (German quantum consortium). Strategically positioned but NOT running insurance-specific quantum applications yet. The door is open.
- **Quantum business case for insurance:** Problems that are computationally impossible for classical computers — catastrophe modeling, cascading risk (storm → infrastructure → supply chain → business interruption → long-term health morbidities). Quantum gives a fundamentally better answer to a different question, not just a faster answer to the same one.
- **The co-development logic:** Insurer does NOT need to build a quantum computer. Engage partnerships (IBM/Google platforms), run small-scale experiments, use quantum-inspired algorithms on classical hardware. Track 2 = strategic patience + learning.

### The Defensive Urgency (Why Insurers Cannot Wait)
- **Harvest Now, Decrypt Later (HNDL) attacks:** Adversaries are already stealing and storing encrypted insurance data TODAY — policyholder records, actuarial models, claims histories — to decrypt once a quantum computer exists. The threat is active NOW, not 2029.
- **Quantum Computing Cybersecurity Preparedness Act (2022):** Already law for 4 years. Establishes federal mandate for PQC migration across critical sectors including financial services and insurance.
- **NIST PQC Standards:** Finalized. FINRA already advising financial firms to engage with new standards immediately.
- **Industry timeline:** Companies targeting 2029–2030 to be quantum-safe. Regulatory outlook: 2031 strategic migration completion target, 2035 full completion, **2030 for critical systems.**
- **The CISO problem:** CISOs have immediate fires today — quantum is deprioritised internally. The winning argument: MindMap's Track 1 is PQC migration (the immediate fire). Track 2 is behavioral intelligence (the remodel). Both delivered together.
- **The 2031 strategic framing:** *"The winning strategy heading into the 2030s isn't about being the first to use quantum, but being the first to be fully secured from it, while keeping your options open on the application side. Secure the house first, then worry about remodeling."* — Quantum Insurance: Strategic Applications & Security Risks (Huxe, May 2026)
- **Offensive vs Defensive R&D split:** The question for insurers isn't just 'how do we use this' but 'how do we survive this.' MindMap answers both tracks simultaneously — PQC protection + behavioral intelligence platform.

### The Perfect Storm Framing
*"It's a perfect storm of strategy. On one hand, you have the promise of revolutionising risk modelling. On the other, the risk of rendering all modern cryptography obsolete."* — Quantum Insurance: Strategic Applications & Security Risks (Huxe, May 2026)
