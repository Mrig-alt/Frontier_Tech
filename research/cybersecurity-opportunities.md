# Cybersecurity as Core Product Opportunity

## 1. AI / GenAI: AI Supply‑Chain Security Platform
*   **The unsolved problem:** Research demonstrates that a small number of poisoned documents can significantly compromise LLM behavior; attackers target AI training pipelines and model registries.
*   **Product concept:** A "SaaS for AI supply-chain security": monitors training data sources, performs data hygiene/provenance checks, scans model registries for anomalies, and integrates with MLOps platforms. Includes incident-response playbooks and regulatory-ready reporting.

## 2. AR / VR: Secure XR Device and Session Management for Enterprises
*   **The unsolved problem:** XR devices introduce new endpoints, sensors, and data types that can be abused to exfiltrate data or physically manipulate users; current EDR/MDM tools are not tailored to XR.
*   **Product concept:** An XR security platform that manages headset identities, enforces secure configurations, monitors sessions for abnormal behavior, and integrates with SIEM/SOAR.

## 3. Blockchain: Smart‑Contract and Web3 Threat‑Hunting Platform
*   **The unsolved problem:** Smart-contract vulnerabilities and address-poisoning attacks drive major losses, and AI agents can now autonomously exploit them.
*   **Product concept:** A security platform that continuously scans on-chain contracts for known patterns and AI-discovered zero-days, simulates attacks, and recommends mitigations.

## 4. Quantum: PQC Migration and Monitoring Suite — MindMap Context
*   **The unsolved problem:** Organizations must replace vulnerable cryptography ahead of quantum breakthroughs; migration is complex and lengthy. For insurance and financial services specifically, **Harvest Now Decrypt Later (HNDL) attacks are already active** — adversaries are stealing and storing encrypted data today to decrypt when quantum computers arrive.
*   **The regulatory mandate:** Quantum Computing Cybersecurity Preparedness Act (2022) — already law, 4 years in force. NIST PQC standards finalized. FINRA advising firms immediately. Critical systems quantum-safe deadline: **2030.** Strategic migration target: 2031. Full completion: 2035.
*   **The CISO tension:** CISOs deprioritise quantum because they have immediate fires to fight. The winning argument: PQC migration IS the immediate fire — HNDL means the breach is happening now. Quantum applications can come after the house is secured.
*   **MindMap's position:** PQC-built from day one. No retrofitting required. This is not a feature — it is the commercial prerequisite that gets MindMap through the door of any insurer or government. Any competitor that hasn't done the migration work is locked out of regulated markets by 2030.
*   **Product concept for MindMap:** PQC layer covers the behavioral data layer (Google Trends + social signals), the quantum-ML engine outputs, and all data-at-rest and in-transit for insurance and government customers. Zero-trust architecture. EU AI Act 2026–27 high-risk compliant. US MIND Act (2025) pathway. Scored separately in the group brief (Criterion 4) because without it, no regulated customer touches the platform.
*   **Why this is a moat:** Platforms that are PQC-compliant in 2026 have a 4-year structural advantage over incumbents that must retrofit before the 2030 deadline. MindMap's co-developer insurer benefits from this advantage directly — their competitors are still exposed.
