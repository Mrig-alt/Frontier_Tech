# Real Company and Industry Pain Points

## 1. Banking and Financial Services
*   **Technology used:** AI/GenAI, Blockchain, Cybersecurity.
*   **Industry/company:** European and global banks.
*   **The unsolved problem:** Legacy systems, siloed data, and payment fragmentation lead to high operational costs, slow product launches, and regulatory risk. Core platforms often exceed 25–30 years in age.
*   **What the product would do:** A combined AI + DLT treasury and data platform consolidating fragmented data, using tokenized assets and real-time payments to reduce settlement friction with embedded cyber controls.
*   **Why now:** Regulatory pressure on resilience, plus the rise of RWA tokenization and open banking.

*   **Role in MindMap system:** Validation engine. Pharma generates peer-reviewed proof that behavioral signals predict real outcomes. This foundation enables insurance and public health to build on science that cannot be disputed.

## 3. Insurance
*   **Technology used:** Quantum ML, AI, Cybersecurity (PQC).
*   **Industry/company:** AXA, Swiss Re, Munich Re equivalents. Mid-to-large global insurers.
*   **The unsolved problem — TWO SIMULTANEOUS THREATS:**

### Offensive Gap (Why Their Model Is Broken)
  Every policy is priced on historical claims — what already happened. Classical actuarial models are structurally backward-looking. Swiss Re has declared AI their #1 strategic priority. AXA's Future Risks Report 2025 names behavioral and social signals as top emerging risk indicators. The gap: no insurer has built an execution vehicle that turns those signals into underwriting intelligence. Munich Re is a founding member of QUTAC (German quantum consortium) — in the room, not executing insurance-specific quantum applications. The door is open.

### Defensive Urgency (Why They Cannot Wait)
  **Harvest Now, Decrypt Later (HNDL) attacks are already live.** Adversaries are actively stealing and storing encrypted policyholder records, actuarial models, and claims histories today — to decrypt once a quantum computer exists. The threat is not theoretical and not 2029. The breach is happening now.
  - Quantum Computing Cybersecurity Preparedness Act (2022): already law, 4 years in force. Establishes mandate for PQC migration across critical sectors including financial services.
  - NIST PQC standards: finalized. FINRA already advising firms to engage immediately.
  - Industry quantum-safe deadline: 2029–2030. Critical systems: **2030 hard deadline.** Strategic migration completion: 2031. Full completion: 2035.
  - The CISO problem: CISOs have immediate fires today — quantum gets deprioritised. MindMap's Track 1 is PQC migration, which is the CISO's immediate fire. This gets MindMap in the door on security grounds alone, before the behavioral intelligence pitch even begins.

*   **What the product would do:** Two-track co-development partnership:
  - **Track 1 (Immediate):** PQC migration of most sensitive data assets — policyholder records, actuarial models. Compliant with Cybersecurity Preparedness Act. Ahead of 2030 critical systems deadline.
  - **Track 2 (H1 pilot → H2 scale):** Behavioral claims prevention study. MindMap's Google Trends + social signal layer, processed through quantum-ML, identifies which behavioral patterns correlate with lower claims. Starts with life & health product line. Not premium discrimination — claims prevention. Consent-based, aggregated, not individual.

*   **Why now:** The winning strategy heading into the 2030s is not about being the first to use quantum — it's about being the first to be fully secured from it, while keeping options open on the application side. MindMap delivers both simultaneously. An insurer that co-develops now builds a 3-year proprietary data advantage before pharma proof is public. By the time competitors catch up, the model is unassailable.

*   **The perfect storm framing:** *"On one hand, the promise of revolutionising risk modelling. On the other, the risk of rendering all modern cryptography obsolete. The question for insurers isn't just how do we use this — but how do we survive this."* — Quantum Insurance: Strategic Applications & Security Risks (Huxe, May 2026)

*   **Why insurance is the commercial anchor (not pharma):** An insurer co-developing MindMap brings regulatory relationships, actuarial credibility, claims data, and domain knowledge. The entity that could block MindMap's approval is the same entity that benefits most from pushing it through. This flips the regulatory risk entirely.

*   **Role in MindMap system:** Commercial engine + regulatory co-developer. Activates publicly once pharma proof arrives, from a position of 3-year unassailable advantage.

The entity that would regulate a neural data platform is the same entity purchasing it. This eliminates the existential regulatory risk that would otherwise threaten the platform.
*   **Why now:** Governments already do this covertly. MindMap is the consent-based, real-time, more accurate version.
*   **Role in MindMap system:** Regulatory risk elimination. The public health vertical is the moat that protects the entire platform from being regulated out of existence.
