# Pharma Stress Test — MindMap

## Why Pharma Was Considered

- Pharma clinical trials are where rigorous, published proof happens. If MindMap's behavioral signals can predict trial outcomes, pharma publishes that proof — and every downstream industry (insurance, public health) can rely on it.
- Clinical trial recruitment is broken: 90%+ of trials fail to recruit the right patients; costs exceed $3B+ per approved drug. This is a real, painful problem with no dominant AI-native solution.
- Pharma has the scientific rigour and budget to be the most demanding and most credible first customer.

## Main Objections Raised and Answers

**Q: Why wouldn't IQVIA just do this themselves?**
A: IQVIA launched IQVIA.ai in March 2026 — they are already building this for large pharma. But small-to-mid biotech running Phase I/II CNS trials cannot afford IQVIA's suite and have no proprietary patient database. That is the market gap MindMap enters. IQVIA optimises for large deal sizes; small CNS biotech is too small for them.

**Q: Is the science ready?**
A: Not fully — but the FDA Biomarker Qualification Program provides a regulatory pathway. The entry product does not need to replace diagnosis; it is a cohort targeting score that reduces trial failure rates. That is a narrow, defensible claim.

**Q: Can a startup compete in pharma?**
A: Alto Neuroscience and Actuate exist as proof that the market is real but incomplete. The entry is not competing with big pharma's R&D — it is selling a targeting tool to small biotech firms that are currently flying blind.

## Stress Test Verdict

**Held up.** Entry product is confirmed as:
- Behavioral cohort targeting score for Phase II CNS trials
- Customer: small-to-mid CNS biotech firms (not IQVIA, not Roche directly at first)
- Value prop: lower trial failure rate using behavioral signals from Google Trends + social media
- Why now: IQVIA is not optimising for this customer segment; the gap is real and accessible

## Final Decision (2026-05-03)

Pharma is locked as the **anchor vertical and validation engine**. The lead teammate owns pharma for both the individual report and the group presentation thesis. Without pharma validating the science, neither insurance nor public health has a credible foundation to build on.
