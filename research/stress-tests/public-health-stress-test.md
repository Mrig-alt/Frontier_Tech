# Public Health Stress Test — MindMap

## Why Public Health Was Considered

- Governments are already spending heavily on understanding voter and citizen sentiment — through polls, focus groups, and social listening. These tools are 2–3 weeks stale and cannot capture real-time emotional states at population scale.
- MindMap gives governments something no poll can: a near-real-time neural-circuit-level map of what their population actually feels, not what they say to a pollster.
- Regulatory risk for a neural data platform is highest in private-sector markets. But if the customer IS the government, the customer controls the regulation — removing the biggest existential risk.

## Main Objections Raised and Answers

**Q: Isn't helping governments map voters unethical?**
A: Governments already do this — polls, Cambridge Analytica, social media monitoring. MindMap is a more accurate, consent-based version of what already happens. The framing is not "manipulation" but "governing better by understanding citizens."

**Q: Won't governments just build this themselves?**
A: Governments are notoriously slow at building internal tech. They outsource intelligence-gathering constantly. The WHO, EU Commission, and national health bodies already buy data products from private vendors. MindMap is a more sophisticated version of what they already purchase.

**Q: Is there a regulation risk?**
A: Yes — but it is mitigated by the customer being the government. The entity that would regulate MindMap is the same entity buying it. This is not a perfect solution but it is the strongest available structural protection for H1.

**Q: How does this connect to the MindMap platform?**
A: Same data layer as pharma and insurance — Google Trends + social media emotional signals fed into quantum ML models. The output is just different: instead of a cohort targeting score or claims prevention model, the output is a population emotional intelligence dashboard.

## Stress Test Verdict

**Held up.** Entry framing confirmed as:
- Population emotional intelligence platform for a named government
- Entry angle: electoral strategy — help government understand how to stay in power by mapping what voters actually feel
- Customer controls regulation = structural moat
- Same MindMap tech stack as pharma and insurance, different output

## Final Decision (2026-05-03)

Public Health is confirmed as the **third vertical**. Teammate 3 owns this vertical. The government-as-customer framing solves the regulation risk that would otherwise threaten a neural data platform operating in the private sector.
