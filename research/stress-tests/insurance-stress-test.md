# Insurance Stress Test — MindMap

## Why Insurance Was Considered

- Insurance is a massive industry built entirely on risk pricing. If MindMap can predict human behavioral risk more accurately than backwards-looking actuarial models, it could structurally disrupt how insurance underwrites policies.
- Insurers like Swiss Re are already calling AI their number one strategic priority — not experimentation, but operating model redesign.
- AXA's Future Risks Report 2025 surveyed 20,000 participants identifying AI, behavioral, and social signals as top emerging risks — exactly the data layer MindMap provides.

## Main Objections Raised and Answers

**Q: Insurance won't touch this until the science is proven. Isn't that a blocker?**
A: Publicly, yes. But a forward-looking insurer doesn't need to wait for public pharma validation. They can run a secret internal pilot NOW — not to sell a product, but to build a proprietary underwriting advantage before any competitor knows the science is ready. By the time pharma publishes validation, that insurer already has 3 years of internal data, a working model, and an unassailable head start.

**Q: What is the actual entry product?**
A: Corrected from initial framing:
- NOT: full neural cognitive risk pricing tool on day one
- YES: behavioral correlation study — show that MindMap's behavioral signals correlate with lower claims or better risk outcomes
- Use case: claims prevention and risk mitigation, not premium discrimination
- Customer: AXA / Swiss Re equivalent running a secret internal pilot

**Q: Why would an insurer trust behavioral signal data over actuarial tables?**
A: They wouldn't replace actuarial tables — they would add MindMap's layer on top as a leading indicator. The moat is the forward-looking behavioral data that backwards-looking actuarial infrastructure cannot replicate.

**Q: Is this ethical / legal?**
A: Entry framing avoids premium discrimination (which is heavily regulated). Claims prevention — helping customers avoid claims through behavioral insight — is a completely different legal frame and is already being explored by major insurers.

## Stress Test Verdict

**Held up — with four corrections applied:**
1. Entry product = behavioral correlation study, not neural pricing
2. Use case = claims prevention, not premium discrimination
3. Neural circuit layer is H2, not H1
4. Moat = forward-looking behavioral data layer, not the quantum ML infrastructure itself

## Final Decision (2026-05-03)

Insurance is confirmed as the **second vertical**. Teammate 2 owns this vertical. The secret pilot logic makes Insurance viable in parallel with pharma — the insurer acts first, before public validation arrives, to build internal advantage.
