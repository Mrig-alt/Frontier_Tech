## Quantum Computing
- **IBM Quantum (Willow chip):** 1,000+ qubits. Demonstrated computational advantage over classical systems in specific problem classes. Key for MindMap's behavioral signal classification engine.
- **IBM + Inclusive Brains joint study (June 2025):** Proves quantum ML can classify neural circuit activation patterns. Direct validation of MindMap's quantum-ML layer for behavioral data.
- **QUTAC (German Quantum Consortium):** Munich Re is a founding member. Industry quantum R&D consortium — confirms insurance sector's quantum positioning but not execution.
- **Quantum-inspired algorithms:** Algorithms that mimic quantum behavior on classical hardware. Relevant for MindMap's H1 deployment before full quantum hardware partnership is in place. Track 2 partnership model (per Huxe episode: Munich Re approach).

## Quantum Threat — Cryptography
- **Harvest Now Decrypt Later (HNDL):** Active adversarial strategy. Encrypted data stolen today, stored, decrypted post-quantum. Insurance and financial services are primary targets due to long data retention requirements.
- **NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) Standards:** Finalized. Mandatory migration path for all critical systems. FINRA advising financial firms to engage now.
- **Quantum Computing Cybersecurity Preparedness Act (2022):** US federal law. 4 years in force as of 2026. Mandates PQC migration across critical sectors. Financial services and insurance fall under scope.
- **2030 / 2031 / 2035 timeline:** Critical systems quantum-safe by 2030. Strategic migration complete 2031. Full completion 2035.

## AI and Behavioral Data
- **Meta TRIBE v2:** 500+ hours fMRI, 700 subjects. 23% improvement in neural encoding. Maps relationship between behavioral inputs and neural responses. MindMap H2+ layer.
- **Google / Harvard brain activity maps:** Population-scale neural mapping research. Contextualises MindMap's behavioral layer as a proxy for population-level cognitive states.
- **AXA Future Risks Report (2025):** Behavioral and social signals identified as top emerging risk indicators. Direct validation of MindMap's insurance thesis.
- **McKinsey / Menlo Ventures enterprise AI ROI study:** 95% of GenAI pilots fail to deliver P&L impact. Supports the "flying blind" problem statement.

## Regulation
- **EU AI Act (2026–2027 enforcement):** High-risk AI classification applies to behavioral data platforms used in health, insurance, and public sector contexts. MindMap's compliance layer is built to this standard.
- **US MIND Act (2025 proposal):** Consent and security requirements for neural and behavioral data. Establishes the US regulatory pathway for MindMap's data layer.
- **FINRA quantum guidance:** FINRA advising financial firms to engage with NIST PQC standards immediately. Supports urgency of Track 1 (PQC migration) in the insurance co-development pitch.
