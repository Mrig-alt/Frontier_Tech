## The Quantum Business Case for Insurance

Classical models: price risk based on historical claims (what already happened).
Quantum models: price risk based on real-time behavioral signals (what is about to happen).

Example — catastrophe modeling:
- Classical: model hurricane's direct path and impact.
- Quantum: simultaneously model storm → infrastructure damage → supply chain disruption → business interruption claims → long-term health morbidities in affected population.
- Result: not a faster answer — a fundamentally better, more holistic answer to a different kind of question.
