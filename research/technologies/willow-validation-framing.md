# Google Willow — External Validation Evidence

**Role in MindMap:** Willow is NOT MindMap infrastructure. It is external evidence that the quantum ML frontier is real and moving fast. It belongs in the research folder, not the architecture.

---

## What Willow Is

- **Owner:** Google Quantum AI
- **Announced:** December 2024
- **Qubits:** 105 superconducting qubits
- **What it demonstrated:** Record random-circuit sampling performance (5 minutes vs. estimated 10^25 years on a classical supercomputer) and below-threshold error correction on logical qubits
- **Access status (as of May 2026):** Not publicly available. Accessible only through Google's Willow Early Access Program — a selective research program for approved scientific experiments, not a commercial cloud service

## What Willow Proves for MindMap's Thesis

Willow demonstrates three things that validate MindMap's directional bet:

1. **The frontier is real.** Google, with the most to lose from antitrust scrutiny, is investing heavily in quantum ML hardware. This is not a theoretical direction.
2. **Error correction is advancing.** Willow's below-threshold logical qubit error correction is the milestone the field needed to credibly talk about fault-tolerant computation on a roadmap.
3. **The window is open.** Being a design partner on quantum-ready pipelines now — before the hardware is general-purpose — is the same bet as building on early cloud infrastructure before AWS was enterprise-grade.

## What Willow Does NOT Prove

- That MindMap runs on Willow
- That quantum ML is production-ready for insurance or pharma workloads today
- That Willow is available for commercial pilots
- That any quantum chip currently provides a proven advantage on behavioral + ICD-10 + claims classification at scale

## How to Reference Willow in the Deck

Correct framing (Frontier Technologies slide):
> "In December 2024, Google announced Willow — a 105-qubit processor that demonstrated record error correction and sampling performance. The quantum ML frontier is moving. MindMap's Layer 2 is built to be quantum-ready when the hardware matures."

Incorrect framing (do not use):
> "Layer 2 uses Google Willow to evaluate the full combinatorial space of behavioral feature interactions."
