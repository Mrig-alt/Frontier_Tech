# Decision Log — MindMap Group Project

This file tracks every major decision made, when it was made, and why. It is the single source of truth for the group's thinking.

---

## 2026-04-23 — Project idea formed

**Decision:** Build a behavioral signal platform that maps human behavior using Google Trends + social media + quantum ML.

**Why:** No dominant product connects behavioral data + quantum ML + neuroscience. Google has the data but hasn't built the bridge to industry. The disruption is to build that bridge before Google or a big incumbent does.

**Platform name:** MindMap

---

## 2026-04-23 — Three industry verticals chosen

**Decision:** Cover three verticals — Pharma, Insurance, Public Health — one per teammate.

**Why:**
- The group brief requires industry disruption at scale. One vertical limits the disruption story.
- The same MindMap data layer can serve all three — it is one platform with three entry points.
- Three verticals also means three meaningfully different individual reports, satisfying the brief requirement.

**Role split:**
- Lead → Pharma (group + individual)
- Teammate 2 → Insurance (individual)
- Teammate 3 → Public Health (individual)

---

## 2026-05-03 — Pharma confirmed as the anchor vertical

**Decision:** Pharma is the primary validation engine. Lead takes Pharma for their individual report.

**Why:**
- Insurance will not price a behavioral risk it cannot dispute — it needs actuarial certainty that only pharma clinical trial data can provide.
- Pharma is where rigorous, published proof happens. Once pharma validates MindMap's behavioral signals, every other industry follows.
- The lead owning Pharma means they own the thesis that holds the entire group presentation together.

**Stress test result:** Held up. Entry product is cohort targeting score for Phase II CNS trials at small-to-mid biotech firms. IQVIA is not a partner — they are a competitor who doesn't serve small biotech. That is the market gap.

---

## 2026-05-03 — Insurance vertical confirmed and stress-tested

**Decision:** Insurance is a valid and important vertical. Teammate 2 takes Insurance.

**Why the secret pilot logic works:**
- A forward-looking insurer (AXA / Swiss Re type) can run a secret internal pilot NOW — before pharma publishes validation — to build 3 years of proprietary behavioral data and get ahead of every competitor.
- By the time pharma proof is public, that insurer already has a working model and an unassailable head start.

**Entry product (corrected):**
- NOT: full neural pricing tool on day one
- YES: behavioral correlation study — show MindMap signals correlate with lower claims / better risk outcomes
- Use case: claims prevention, not premium discrimination
- Customer: AXA / Swiss Re equivalent

**Stress test result:** Held up. Swiss Re and AXA are already moving toward AI-driven behavioral risk modeling. The market is ready. MindMap's moat is the forward-looking behavioral data layer that backwards-looking actuarial infrastructure cannot replicate.

---

## 2026-05-03 — Public Health vertical confirmed

**Decision:** Public Health is the third vertical. Teammate 3 takes it.

**Why:**
- Governments already use polls and social listening. MindMap gives them real-time population-level behavioral intelligence — something no poll can match.
- The entry angle: help governments map voter/population sentiment to understand how to stay in power.
- Strategic advantage: the customer (government) controls the regulation, removing one of the biggest risks for a neural data platform.
