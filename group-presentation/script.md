## SLIDE 4 — Why These Three Industries

- **Title:** The Logic Behind Each Vertical
- **Body:**

  **Pharma (lead)**
  - Pharma is the validation engine. Clinical trials are where rigorous proof happens.
  - If MindMap's behavioral signals predict trial outcomes, pharma publishes the proof — and every other industry follows.
  - Entry: cohort targeting score for Phase II CNS trials at small-to-mid biotech firms

  **Insurance (teammate 2)**
  - Insurance will not price a risk it cannot dispute — it needs pharma validation first.
  - But a forward-looking insurer can run a secret internal pilot NOW, before the science goes public, to build 3 years of proprietary data and get ahead of every competitor.
  - Entry: behavioral correlation study → claims prevention model at AXA or Swiss Re equivalent

  **Public Health (teammate 3)**
  - Governments already use polls, focus groups, social listening. MindMap gives them something no poll can: a real-time population sentiment map.
  - Entry: government emotional intelligence platform for electoral strategy — customer controls the regulation
