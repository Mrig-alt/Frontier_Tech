# MindMap Market Opportunity by Vertical

## What This Chart Shows
A grouped bar chart comparing Total Addressable Market (TAM) against the addressable entry market for each of MindMap's three verticals.

## Market Sizing

| Vertical | TAM | Entry Market | Entry Customer Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pharma (CNS Trials) | $45B | $1.2B | Small-to-mid CNS biotech; Phase II trial cohort targeting |
| Insurance (L&H Risk) | $78B | $3.5B | AXA / Swiss Re equivalent; behavioral correlation study → claims prevention |
| Public Health (Gov) | $22B | $0.8B | Named government; population sentiment dashboard pilot |

## Why Insurance Has the Largest Entry Market
Insurance's $3.5B entry market is the largest of the three despite not having the largest TAM. This is because:
1. The insurer does not need to wait for external scientific validation — it runs an internal pilot and owns the results
2. The product (behavioral correlation study → claims prevention model) maps directly to existing actuarial budget lines
3. Unlike pharma (FDA approval pathway) or public health (government procurement cycles), insurance can move on a commercial contract alone

This is the core commercial argument for Insurance as the anchor vertical — not that the market is biggest, but that the path to first revenue is fastest and most controllable.

## Source Note
Market sizing figures are directional estimates based on research conducted during project stress-testing sessions. See `research/stress-tests/` for full sourcing and methodology.
