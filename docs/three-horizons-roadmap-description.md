# MindMap Three Horizons Roadmap

## What This Chart Shows
A Gantt-style horizontal bar chart mapping every milestone across MindMap's three verticals — Pharma, Insurance, and Public Health — across three time horizons.

## Milestone Detail

### H1 — Now to 12 Months
| Vertical | Milestone |
|---|---|
| Pharma | Behavioral cohort targeting score; Phase II CNS trial pilot at small CNS biotech |
| Insurance | Secret internal behavioral correlation pilot at one insurer (Aurora-type target) |
| Public Health | Population sentiment dashboard pilot for one named government |

### H2 — 12 to 24 Months
| Vertical | Milestone |
|---|---|
| Pharma | Proof of concept published; other pharma firms begin adoption; quantum ML biomarker scoring live |
| Insurance | Pilot internal results validate; expand to full underwriting model; co-development agreement formalised |
| Public Health | WHO / EU Commission entry; government contracts expand beyond pilot |

### H3 — 24 Months and Beyond
| Vertical | Milestone |
|---|---|
| Pharma | MindMap becomes behavioral data standard in clinical trials globally |
| Insurance | New actuarial modeling standard incorporating behavioral signal layer |
| Public Health | Population health policy standard; neural circuit layer added (Meta TRIBE v2, Harvard/Google maps) |

## Strategic Logic of the Sequence
H1 is designed so all three pilots run simultaneously but independently — no vertical depends on another's H1 success. The insurance pilot is "secret" by design (strategic pilot framing) because insurers cannot publicly admit they are exploring behavioral risk modeling before regulatory approval. Pharma's H1 proof of concept is what unlocks H2 for all three verticals — the science validation from pharma is the shared foundation.
