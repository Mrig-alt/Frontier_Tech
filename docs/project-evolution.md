# MindMap — Project Evolution

This document captures how the project idea developed from first principles to the confirmed three-vertical structure. It is the "meta-story" of the project: not what we decided, but how we got there.

---

## Round 1: The Core Insight (April 2026)

**Starting point:** Google is sitting on the most detailed map of human anxiety ever assembled (136B monthly searches vs ChatGPT's ~4B). Nobody has built the bridge from that behavioral exhaust to industry applications.

**Thesis formed:** Aggregate consented behavioral signals (Google Trends + social media) at population scale, feed hybrid quantum-ML models, and sell the output as causal cognitive intelligence to industries that currently make decisions blind.

**Platform named:** MindMap

**First disruption frame:** Mental health / neuropharmaceuticals / cognitive insurance — industries where diagnosis is still subjective and behavioral data could replace guesswork.

---

## Round 2: Vision 1 vs Vision 2 (April 2026)

Two parallel visions were developed and stress-tested:

**Vision 1 — Predictive Cognitive Intelligence Platform**
- Use behavioral + neural signals to predict human cognitive and emotional states
- Commercial entry: pharma trials, insurance underwriting, public health policy
- Horizon 1: government compliance tool (EU AI Act / DSA)
- Horizon 2: quantum ML biomarker scoring for pharma and insurance
- Horizon 3: new standard of care across all three industries

**Vision 2 — Cognitive Foundry (Synthetic Embodiment)**
- Use brain mapping to eventually direct lab-grown organoids or synthetic bodies
- Inspired by Boston Dynamics + Hyundai robotic integration angle
- Horizon 1: brain-computer interface data collection
- Horizon 2: cognitive blueprint from behavioral data
- Horizon 3: synthetic human body directed by a lab-grown brain organoid

**Decision:** Vision 1 chosen as the group project anchor — it has a cleaner commercial entry, a defensible H1 product, and a realistic roadmap. Vision 2 is a moonshot that would be H3 territory at best.

---

## Round 3: Realising the Brief is INDUSTRY Disruption (April 2026)

**Problem identified:** Early decks were pitching the MindMap platform as a product. But the group brief says: *"Future Strategic Opportunities to Transform or Disrupt Your Industry."* The argument must be that an entire industry cannot survive the next 5 years unchanged — and MindMap is the disruptive force.

**Reframe applied:**
- Not: "Here is a product we want to build"
- But: "Here is why this industry is about to be structurally disrupted — and here is where the strategic opening is"

This reframe changed the entire presentation structure: lead with the industry's structural fragility, then introduce MindMap as the disruptor.

---

## Round 4: Three Verticals From One Platform (April–May 2026)

**Decision made:** Instead of one vertical, use three — one per teammate — all drawing from the same MindMap data layer.

**Why:**
- The brief requires the group presentation to be meaningfully different from individual reports
- Three verticals let the group deck argue macro disruption while each individual report zooms into one customer
- The same behavioral signal layer (Google Trends + social media + quantum ML) powers all three

**Role split:**
- Lead → Pharma (individual report + leading the group presentation)
- Teammate 2 → Insurance (individual report)
- Teammate 3 → Public Health (individual report)

---

## Round 5: Pharma as the Anchor (May 2026)

**Logic confirmed:** Pharma is not just one vertical — it is the **validation engine** that makes the other two possible.

- Insurance will not price a behavioral risk it cannot dispute. That certainty comes from pharma clinical trial proof.
- Public health tools need credible science behind the behavioral signals.
- Therefore: pharma must succeed first. The lead owning pharma means they own the thesis the whole group presentation is built on.

**Lead strategy:** Build pharma individual report first as the template. Insurance and public health reports borrow the structure and apply it to their verticals.

---

## Round 6: Insurance Reframed as Secret Pilot (May 2026)

**Problem:** Insurance needs pharma validation before it can act publicly. That makes it look like a dependent H2 play with no H1 traction.

**Reframe:** A specific forward-looking insurer can run a **secret internal pilot** right now — not to publish, but to build proprietary underwriting advantage before competitors know the science is ready. By the time pharma proof is public, that insurer already has 3 years of data and an unassailable head start.

This reframe makes insurance viable as a genuine parallel H1 play alongside pharma, not just a dependent future state.

---

## Round 7: Public Health as Electoral Intelligence (May 2026)

**Framing confirmed:** Not "help governments improve citizen wellbeing" (generic and weak) but "give governments a real-time population emotional intelligence map to understand and influence their voters."

**Key structural advantage:** The customer (government) controls the regulation — removing the biggest existential risk for a neural data platform.

---

- All references to the old internal working name removed
- Platform consistently called **MindMap** across all files, slides, and documents
- Repository structure confirmed: group-presentation, individual (pharma/insurance/public-health), research, docs, platform-architecture
