# Individual Report Outline: MindMap — Insurance Vertical

> This is the lead's insurance-angle outline, used for context and cross-referencing the group deck.
> The lead's individual report is Pharma. This file captures the insurance framing developed during group work.

---

## 1. Problem Statement

- Insurers price risk using backwards-looking actuarial models built on historical claims data.
- They have no real-time view of how population behavior is shifting — which means they are always one cycle behind on emerging risk.
- Behavioral data exists at scale (Google Trends, social signals) but no secure, regulated, insurer-ready platform has bridged it to underwriting.
- The regulatory environment (EIOPA, FCA, Solvency II, EU AI Act) makes it impossible to just plug a consumer tech API into underwriting — the trust and compliance layer has to be built in from day one.

---

## 2. Solution: MindMap Insurance Vertical

- MindMap's behavioral signal layer feeds population-level early-risk signals into the insurer's underwriting workflow.
- It is not a pricing tool on day one. It is a behavioral correlation study — showing that MindMap signals predict claims events before they happen.
- The entry product is a secret internal pilot at one forward-looking insurer (AXA / Swiss Re equivalent).
- Core value proposition: get 3 years of proprietary behavioral-claims data before any competitor knows this is possible.

---

## 3. Technology Stack

- **AI / Classical ML (H1):** Behavioral signal processing and correlation scoring against claims outcomes. No quantum needed at this stage.
- **Quantum ML (H2):** Once real claims data exists, quantum-classical hybrid models (QUBO-type optimization) improve pattern separation in high-dimensional behavioral data. IBM Quantum AI / Willow chip.
- **Cybersecurity (all horizons):** PQC-ready encryption, zero-trust architecture, differential privacy on population-level data, full audit logging. EU AI Act high-risk classification compliant. This is the trust layer that makes insurer adoption possible — not a compliance checkbox.

---

## 4. Three Horizons Roadmap (Insurance-Specific)

- **H1 (Now):** Bespoke early-risk signals for underwriting. One insurer, secret pilot, behavioral correlation scores delivered. Proves the signal has predictive value.
- **H2 (12–24 months):** Precision from real claims outcomes. The pilot's claims data validates and improves the model. Quantum ML enters as an accelerator. Insurer begins integrating outputs into underwriting decisions.
- **H3 (24+ months):** The data moat becomes the platform. Years of proprietary claims-linked behavioral data make the model harder to replicate. Switching cost compounds. MindMap becomes the insurer's long-term behavioral intelligence layer.

---

## 5. Strategic Advantage

- The moat is not the technology — it is the **data flywheel**. The longer MindMap runs with a specific insurer, the more claims-linked behavioral data it accumulates, and the harder it becomes to replicate or replace.
- Cybersecurity is the commercial enabler: without PQC-ready, zero-trust, audit-ready infrastructure, no regulated insurer will allow this into their underwriting workflow.
- First-mover advantage is time-sensitive: Swiss Re and AXA are already moving toward AI-driven behavioral risk modeling. The window to enter as the trusted data layer — before a big tech player does — is open now, not in 3 years.

---

## 6. Key Q&A Answers

- **"Why insurance if pharma hasn't validated yet?"** — The insurer acts in secret now precisely because pharma hasn't published yet. By the time pharma proof is public, they already have 3 years of internal data and a working model.
- **"Where does quantum fit?"** — H2. Enough real data exists by then for quantum to add meaningful precision. We are not claiming quantum is required on day one.
- **"Isn't this just AI?"** — Classical ML is the H1 tool. Quantum is the H2 accelerator. The strategic value is the behavioral data layer + cybersecurity trust layer, not the algorithm alone.
