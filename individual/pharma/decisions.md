# Pharma Vertical — Decision Log

## Role
Lead teammate. Owns both the individual report and leads the group presentation.

---

## 2026-05-03 — Pharma confirmed as anchor vertical

- Pharma is where published proof happens. If MindMap's behavioral signals predict clinical outcomes, pharma publishes that — and insurance and public health follow.
- Insurance needs actuarial certainty before it will price a behavioral risk. Only pharma clinical data creates that certainty.
- Decision: Lead takes pharma. This is not just an individual report topic — it is the thesis the entire group deck is built on.

## 2026-05-03 — Entry customer confirmed: small-to-mid CNS biotech

- Big pharma (Roche, J&J) and CROs (IQVIA) are too advanced and competitive — IQVIA launched IQVIA.ai in March 2026.
- Small-to-mid CNS biotech firms running Phase I/II trials cannot afford IQVIA's suite, have no proprietary patient database, and are open to novel targeting approaches in exchange for lower price and genuine scientific partnership.
- That is the market gap MindMap enters.

## 2026-05-03 — Entry product confirmed: cohort targeting score

- NOT: diagnosis replacement or full trial management platform
- YES: a behavioral cohort targeting score that reduces Phase II trial failure rates
- The score uses MindMap's Google Trends + social media signals to identify which patient populations are most likely to complete and respond to a CNS trial
- Regulatory pathway: FDA Biomarker Qualification Program

## 2026-05-03 — Business model framing

- B2B sale to small CNS biotech
- Priced as a service (per-trial targeting score), not a platform licence
- The commercial goal at H1 is not revenue — it is generating publishable proof of concept that the behavioral signal layer works
- That proof is what makes insurance and public health viable at H2
