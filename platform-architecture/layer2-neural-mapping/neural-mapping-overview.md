# Layer 2 — Neural Mapping & Behavioral Model Layer

## What This Layer Does

Layer 2 is the scientific translation engine of the MindMap platform. It takes the normalised population-level behavioral signals from Layer 1 and maps them to validated brain-state and behavioral models — producing structured, industry-ready behavioral indicators that feed the quantum ML engine in Layer 3.

This is where raw search signal becomes **predictive intelligence**: the layer that answers *what these behaviors mean* in clinical, actuarial, and epidemiological terms.

---

## Scientific Foundation

### Google / Harvard Brain Connectome (2024)
- **What it is:** The most detailed map of a piece of human brain tissue ever produced
- **Scale:** 1 cubic millimetre of human brain tissue; 1.4 petabytes of data; 57,000 cells; 150 million synapses
- **Published:** May 2024 (Google Research + Harvard University)
- **Relevance to MindMap:** Provides the neural substrate reference layer — the biological map of how human behavioral patterns are encoded in brain architecture
- **What it enables:** MindMap can anchor its behavioral signal taxonomy to empirically validated neural structures, not just correlational statistics

### EBRAINS Human Brain Atlas
- **What it is:** A multi-scale human brain atlas built by the EU Human Brain Project
- **Coverage:** Whole-brain activity mapping; cellular-level resolution in key regions
- **Relevance to MindMap:** Provides the **activity mapping overlay** — which brain regions activate in response to which classes of behavioral stimuli
- **Partnership model (H2):** EBRAINS operates as an open research infrastructure; MindMap targets a data access partnership for the pharma vertical

### Validated Behavioral Biomarker Literature
- **Source:** PubMed cross-reference layer from Layer 1
- **Function:** Maps specific search-signal clusters (e.g., anxiety-related queries, pain-related queries, mental health-related queries) to peer-reviewed behavioral biomarkers
- **Validation standard:** Only biomarkers with published clinical evidence are used in the production model


## The Industry Stress Test: Why This Layer Drives Vertical Sequencing

The neural mapping layer was the basis for the industry selection decision (Insurance first, then Pharma, then Public Health). The stress test evaluated all three verticals against five criteria:

| Criterion | Insurance | Pharma | Public Health |
|---|---|---|---|
| Science maturity of behavioral signals | High | Very High | High |
| Commercial anchor potential | **Highest** | Medium | Low (long procurement) |
| Regulatory readiness | **Highest** | Medium | Low (political sensitivity) |
| Data availability (H1) | **Highest** | Medium | Medium |
| Time to first revenue | **Shortest** | Longer | Longest |

**Insurance scored highest on the dimensions that matter for H1 commercial execution.** Pharma scores highest on science maturity (the behavioral biomarker evidence base is strongest for clinical applications) but requires longer validation cycles before revenue. Public Health is the scale play, not the anchor.

## What This Layer Is Not

- **Not individual tracking:** All mapping is done at population-aggregate level. No individual behavioral profiles are constructed or stored.
- **Not raw neuroscience:** MindMap does not run brain scans. It uses the connectome and atlas as *reference models* to validate that its behavioral signal taxonomy maps to empirically grounded neural structures.
- **Not a medical device:** The behavioral indicators produced by this layer are strategic intelligence inputs for industry decision-makers, not clinical diagnostic tools.

## Regulatory Alignment

| Regulation | Relevance | MindMap Position |
|---|---|---|
| EU AI Act (2024) | Cognitive and behavioral data requires explicit consent architecture and high-risk AI classification review | Consent architecture built into Layer 4 (Security & Governance); population-level aggregation avoids individual high-risk classification |
| US MIND Act | Neural data sovereignty; restricts commercial use of individual neural data | MindMap uses population-level indexed signals, not individual neural data — compliant by design |
| EU DSA (2025) | Platforms must assess mental harm from algorithmic outputs | MindMap outputs are B2B intelligence, not consumer-facing algorithmic decisions — outside direct DSA scope; monitored for downstream use |
