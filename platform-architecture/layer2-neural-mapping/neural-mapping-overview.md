# Layer 2 — Neural Mapping & Behavioral Output Correlation

## What This Layer Does

Layer 2 takes the population-level behavioral signals from Layer 1 and maps them to validated brain-state and behavioral science models. It is the translation layer — converting raw signal indices into meaningful behavioral constructs (anxiety, avoidance, health-seeking behaviour, financial stress) that carry predictive power for industry outcomes.

---

## The Science Foundation

### Google / Harvard Brain Connectome (2024)

In 2024, Google Research and Harvard University published a complete map of a 1 cubic millimetre section of the human brain:

- **1.4 petabytes** of data
- **57,000 cells** mapped
- **150 million synapses** traced

This represents the most detailed neural substrate map ever produced. It provides the scientific foundation for understanding how external stimuli (fears, desires, health concerns) translate into observable behavioral outputs — and by extension, into search behaviour.

> This is the scientific credibility layer. MindMap does not claim to read individual minds. It claims that population-level behavioral signals (what people search for) correlate with known neurological patterns of anxiety, health-seeking, and risk response — patterns that are now mappable.

### EBRAINS Atlas

The EBRAINS Human Brain Atlas provides activity mapping overlays — validated neurological activity patterns corresponding to cognitive and emotional states. Layer 2 uses EBRAINS atlas data as a cross-reference for signal-to-behavior mapping.

## How Behavioral Mapping Works

The mapping process translates search signal clusters into behavioral constructs:

| Search Signal Cluster | Behavioral Construct | Industry Application |
|---|---|---|
| Symptom searches + health anxiety terms | Health-seeking behaviour intensity | Insurance claims prevention signal |
| Financial stress terms + debt-related searches | Financial vulnerability index | Life insurance risk scoring |
| CNS condition searches + treatment queries | Neurological symptom onset pattern | Pharma trial cohort identification |
| Outbreak-related terms + geographic clustering | Population disease anxiety | Public health early warning |

## Industry Stress Test Rationale

The Layer 2 mapping was the basis for the industry selection decision. The stress test evaluated all three verticals (Insurance, Pharma, Public Health) against five criteria:

1. **Signal-to-outcome correlation strength** — How directly do behavioral signals predict measurable outcomes?
2. **Existing data availability** — Can we cross-validate signals against known outcome data?
3. **Regulatory safety of signal use** — Are consent and privacy frameworks adequate?
4. **Commercial partner willingness** — Will industry partners share outcome data for model training?
5. **Time to first proof point** — How long until we can demonstrate predictive validity?

**Result:** Insurance (Life & Health) scored highest on criteria 1, 2, and 4. This drove the decision to enter Insurance as the commercial anchor.

## What Layer 2 Is Not

- It does **not** track individuals
- It does **not** diagnose conditions
- It does **not** make clinical claims
- It produces **population-level behavioral indices** that carry statistical predictive power — not individual assessments

This distinction is what keeps the platform within EU AI Act compliance and outside the high-risk AI classification that would require individual-level consent at point of inference.

## Connection to Layer 3

Layer 2 outputs structured behavioral feature vectors — multi-dimensional signal composites — that are fed into the Layer 3 quantum-ML engine. The quantum engine's advantage is precisely in evaluating the combinatorial interactions between these feature vectors at a scale and complexity that classical ML cannot match.
