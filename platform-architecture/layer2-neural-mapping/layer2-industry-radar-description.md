# Layer 2 — Industry Vertical Readiness Radar

## What This Chart Shows
A 6-dimension radar (spider) chart comparing MindMap's three target verticals — Pharma, Insurance, and Public Health — across the stress-tested readiness criteria from the project decision sessions.

## Scores (out of 10)

| Dimension | Pharma | Insurance | Public Health |
|---|---|---|---|
| Market Size | 8 | 7 | 6 |
| Regulatory Readiness | 6 | 8 | 5 |
| Data Availability | 9 | 7 | 8 |
| Commercial Anchor | 7 | 9 | 5 |
| Science Maturity | 9 | 7 | 6 |
| Speed to Revenue | 6 | 8 | 5 |

## Key Decision Insight
Insurance scores highest on **Commercial Anchor (9/10)** because an insurer that co-develops MindMap has the most to gain and the most regulatory power to push it through. This is not just a first customer — it is a co-development partner with actuarial credibility, regulatory relationships, and a direct financial incentive to make the science stick.

Pharma scores highest on **Science Maturity (9/10)** — this is why Pharma remains the scientific validation vertical even though Insurance is the commercial anchor. The two roles are complementary, not competing.
